# Corpus audit — 24 Oct 2026

Scope: the 76 Markdown files behind the reader site (74 `corpus/*.md` + `corpus/vasconcelos/*.md`, 2 `corpus/resources/*.md`), plus `corpus/README.md`, `corpus/PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `glossaries/`. Commit 7bd1275 (local copy = published).
Method: scripted checks over every file (front matter, page-marker sequences, block pairing, language of each block, fragmentation, OCR noise, tags), then hand inspection of everything flagged and random original/English pairs from each edition. Counts are from the scripts; "e.g." items were checked by eye.

The good news first: in the page-file editions (Bardili, Bolzano, Boutroux, Brentano, Dilthey, Exner, Fries, Göschel, Henríquez Ureña, Rosenkranz, Varona ×2, Klages *Geist*, Trendelenburg chapters) original and English pair up block for block. The median English/original length ratio is 0.93–1.02, no blocks look dropped, numbers match in >99% of pairs, and the sampled translations are faithful. Every file is listed in the README and on the site.

## A. Critical — wrong content or wrong citations

### A1. Hegel, *Enzyklopädie* 1817: German and English are out of step from p. 146 to the end
On 95 of 303 pages the § numbers in the quoted German do not match the § numbers in the English on the same page. From p. 146 the German runs ahead, by 2 §§ at first and by about 10 §§ near the end. Examples:
- p. 171: German §§ 252–253, English §§ 246–247.
- p. 225: German §§ 343–346, English §§ 333–335.
- p. 283: German §§ 475–477, English §§ 465–467.

The German beside each English paragraph is therefore a different passage (e.g. p. 171: German on metals and oxides, English on colour, smell and taste). Some pages also look scrambled, with words run together ("Spannungund", "Bildungdes").
Also: the markers `[p. 141]` (between 113 and 115) and `[p. 229]` (between 221 and 223) are misread folios, probably 114 and 222. And p. 286 has a library date-slip ("DAY OVERDUE … $1.00") OCR'd into a German block.
**Fix:** re-cut the OCR from the page files for pp. 146–302 (or from the scans) and re-export. Until then, warn in the header that the German from p. 146 on does not line up with the English.

### A3. English broken at every printed line in the PDF-recovered editions
The English here was taken from the PDF line by line, so paragraphs break at each printed line, often mid-word ("tran‐ ¶ scribing"). Typesetting debris also became text: catchwords ("purchased" ¶ [p. 24] ¶ "purchased at a great price…"), running heads and signatures ("History of Mexico, Vol. I.  D  because"), split drop caps ("W       herever"), letter-spaced titles ("O F RU E DA , D O C T O R"), and printed folios as text lines ("— 166 —").

| rubio-logica-mexicana | 5,257 | 1,428 | — | 114 |
| gamarra-elementa | 4,686 | 923 | 165 | 90 (+275 drop-cap splits) |
| clavigero-1 | 3,386 | 604 | 98 | 13 |
| clavigero-3 | 3,174 | 588 | 86 | 13 |

| clavigero-4 | 240 | 13 | 58 | 3 |

| palagyi-ismerettan | 136 | 32 | — | — |
| hostos-tratado | 121 | 30 | — | 63 |

This hurts every use of the corpus. `grep` misses phrases that straddle a line, the Claude Project retrieves fragments, and the reader shows broken cards.
**Fix:** a reflow pass. Join a paragraph to the next when it ends without terminal punctuation and the next starts lowercase. Re-join `‐`/`-` + newline splits. Delete a catchword paragraph when it repeats the first word after the next marker. Delete running-head and signature lines. Turn `— N —` lines into `[p. N]` (Bachofen) or delete them. Re-join drop caps and letter-spacing.

## B. Consistency — the files don't follow one format

### B1. Five shapes, but the docs describe one
README and PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS say every file has "the original in quoted blocks, the English in plain paragraphs". In fact the files come in five shapes:
1. **Paragraph-paired original + English**: the 15 page-file editions, Klages *Geist*, Trendelenburg chapters.
2. **One OCR blob per page + English paragraphs**: Luz y Caballero, Rubio, Vaz Ferreira. The original is not cut at paragraphs (median per-block length ratio 0.25 / 0.15 / 0.17). `## ` headings sit *inside* the `>` blockquote, and only part of the original is present (Luz: 362 original blocks vs 1,869 English; Vaz Ferreira 222 vs 1,202).
3. **English only, original "not in this file"**: Bachofen, Bello, Clavigero ×4, Gamarra ×2, Hostos, Klages *Errungenschaften*, Palágyi ×2, Tiberghien, Wolff (14).
4. **Original only, as plain paragraphs (not quoted)**: 37 Vasconcelos, Dacqué, Obereit, Klages *Persönlichkeit*, Trendelenburg LU I–II originals (42).
5. **Resources** (study guide, concordance): no front matter at all.

**Fix:** document all five in README and PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS. Add an `original_text` value for shape 2 ("whole-page OCR, not cut at paragraphs"). Move `## ` headings out of blockquotes.

### B2. The "Conventions" boilerplate contradicts the header
- In **59** files it says "Generated file — see the header for where corrections belong", but the header says "THIS FILE IS THE SOURCE, corrections go here".
- In **14** files (shape 3) it says "quoted blocks are the original" when there are none.
- In **42** files (shape 4) it says "plain paragraphs the English" when the plain paragraphs are the original.
- It never mentions `[scan N]` / `[PDF N]` (B3) or inline markers (clean-text editions).

**Fix:** generate the Conventions line from the header fields in `corpus.py`.

### B3. Page-marker scheme varies
- `[scan N]` / `[PDF N]` (positions in the PDF) are mixed with `[p. N]` and documented nowhere. Some files have no printed-page markers at all:
  - Henríquez Ureña: 138 `[scan]`, 0 `[p.]`
  - Hostos: 273 `[PDF]`
  - *Juventuti Americanae*: 15 `[PDF]`
  - Bachofen: 448 `[PDF]` + 2 `[p.]`, with the printed folios in the text as "— 166 —"

  The citation rule in PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS ("cite the printed page, use the nearest `[p. N]`") can't be followed in these files.
- **Multi-volume numbering:**
  - Exner uses `[p. I.12]` / `[p. II.5]` (good).
  - **Fries** restarts at p. 1 for vol. II with no volume prefix (311 duplicate page numbers), so a citation like "p. 8" is ambiguous.
  - **Gamarra** restarts at 1 several times (227 duplicates).
  - Klages *Geist* runs continuously.

  Pick one scheme (`[p. II.8]`).
- Placement differs: own line (photocopy editions), inline in both columns (clean-text), inline in the original only (Vasconcelos). That's fine, but document it.
- `pages:` means different things. It is sometimes the marker count, sometimes the book length (Klages *Geist* 1369 vs ~1,288 markers; Boutroux 20 vs ~20 inline). *El realismo científico* has `pages: 0`.

### B4. Front-matter gaps and style drift
- **Empty `year`:** Luz y Caballero, Vasconcelos *Lógica orgánica*, *Discursos 1920–1950*. The README then prints "*Lógica Orgánica* ()".
- **Empty `title_en`:** all 37 Vasconcelos files, including the 30 Spanish ones.
- **Year formats:** "1859 (2nd ed. 1925)" gives nested brackets in the title line and README. Ranges are written 1842–1844 / 1929–1932 (en dash), 1910-11 (hyphen), and 1919/20 / 1940/50 (slash). Choose `year` + optional `edition_year`, with en-dash ranges.
- **YAML quoting** varies: Klages *Geist* (`original_text`, `translation`), Clavigero IV (`title`, `title_en`), Vasconcelos *Pitágoras* and *Education in Mexico* (`title`).
- **Author:** "Melchior Palágyi" (German book) vs "Menyhért Palágyi" (Hungarian book). Both are correct, but the index and grouping treat them as two people. Add a shared sort key, or use one form with the other in parentheses.
- **`generated:`:** Klages *Geist* says "from the parallel-edition PDF (extract.py / assemble.py)"; all others say "by corpus.py".

### B5. Glossaries
15 of 33 translated editions have a glossary. None exists for the 14 PDF-recovered editions, Luz y Caballero, Rubio, Vaz Ferreira or the Trendelenburg chapters. PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS rule 3 assumes every book has one. Either write minimal glossaries for the 18, or reword rule 3 to say "where a glossary exists".

### B6. Untranslated quotations: no single policy
Latin, Greek, Italian and French quotations are sometimes left untranslated in the English column and sometimes translated:
- Left untranslated: Bachofen (6 long Latin/Italian passages), Brentano (9 Latin Aquinas/Averroes notes).
- Translated or glossed: Göschel (Latin given with its translation).

Decide on one policy (e.g. keep the quotation, then add the English in brackets) and apply it.

### B7. PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS is out of date
- It describes the group as "Kant and his aftermath", but 46 of 74 files are Latin American philosophy.
- It describes only shape 1 (B1) and doesn't mention `[scan]` / `[PDF]` markers, volume prefixes, original-only files, or the two `resources/` files.
- It doesn't warn that Klages *Geist* is "unrevised: not reviewed against the original" (the only such file). That status is in the file header but not in the README table. Add a "review status" column.

## C. OCR quality of the originals (known, but where it matters most)
The headers already say "uncorrected", but these are bad enough to mislead a `grep` or a quotation:
- **Rubio**: 30,067 `ſ`, run-together words ("Lorumquafunt,aliadefubiectoaliquodicuntur"), and Cyrillic look-alikes ("САРУТ").
- **Bardili**: 11,344 `ſ` and ~420 Fraktur misreads ("ih", "nihts", "$antifd)en", "Ldflund").
- **Bolzano**: 15,097 `ſ` (accurate long-s, but `grep "Theorie"` won't match "Theorie" written with ſ).
- **Brentano**: ~730 misread tokens.
- **Hegel**: "Gesektseyn", "Orid", "Gegensakes".

**Fix:** at minimum, add a search-normalised copy (ſ→s, ꝛ→r, rejoin hyphens) or document that the search should use ſ. Full correction can wait.
