# Corpus — facing editions

One folder per edition, `corpus/<slug>/`: `edition.yaml` (the header: title, languages, where the texts come from and how far they have been checked, plus the `# ` heading and the `Conventions:` paragraph), `original.md` and `en.md` (one language each, `[p. N]` at every printed page start; headings, `⁂` and standalone page marks stand in both; an original-only text has `original.md` only), and, when they exist, `glossary.md` and `notes.md`. `tools/reader.py` merges the two languages into the facing text (original in quoted blocks, English in plain paragraphs) and writes it to the site as `facing/<name>.md`; those generated facing files, downloadable from the site, are what the Claude Project takes, not `corpus/*.md`. A new edition that arrives as one interleaved file goes through `python3 tools/split.py corpus/<name>.md` (`export_all.sh` stages its output in `staging/` and runs `tools/split.py --into corpus` on it, which skips every edition that already has a folder); `reader.py` also reads such a file as it is, which is how the two Trendelenburg *Logische Untersuchungen* originals (`trendelenburg-lu-1-original.md`, `trendelenburg-lu-2-original.md`) are still kept: their footnotes are quoted blocks, which a folder cannot hold. All translations are machine-made (Claude) and reviewed against the original by Claude, not by a human translator, unless the header says otherwise. The folder is the source: corrections go into `corpus/<slug>/` (`original.md`, `en.md`, `edition.yaml`); nothing regenerates it, and `export_all.sh` only adds editions that have no folder yet.

Paragraph standard (editions whose `edition.yaml` says `paragraphs: printed`; the others are paired page by page): `original.md` holds the printed paragraphs, never cut at a page turn, `[p. N]` inline where the page begins; `en.md` has exactly one unit (paragraph or heading) per original unit, same order, `[p. N]` at the matching point; so paragraph n of one file pairs with paragraph n of the other. The reader cuts each pair at its shared `[p. N]` marks for display, one card per printed page. The mark may also be a standard edition's page, `[AA 4:388]` (Akademie-Ausgabe, volume:page); an edition with an original only and `paragraphs: printed` (the Kant volumes) is cut the same way, one card per page, and its own notes (`[^id]: *) …`) stand under the page that holds their call.
