# Glossary and policies — Bachofen, *Versuch über die Gräbersymbolik der Alten* (2nd ed. 1925) → *Essay on the Tomb Symbolism of the Ancients*

Decisions recorded during production; partial.

- **Greek and Latin quotations**: kept untranslated, as printed.
- **Greek misprints**: a non-word misprint is given in the standard text; a real word or variant reading is kept as the print has it (the same rule for Latin).
- **Scope**: the whole book, including the Bernoulli and Klages prefaces of 1925, the key to the plates, the Übersicht, the index and the plates.
- **Index**: headwords translated, German alphabetical order kept.
- **Footnotes**: marks as printed (1), 2), …); Greek verse lines in notes set as verse.
- **Catchwords/signatures**: the printer's signature numerals (every 16 pages) are omitted in the English.
