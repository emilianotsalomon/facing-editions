## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: the second edition, Basel 1925, Fraktur, Google Books scan of the Berkeley copy (472 pages); the scans are 1-bit. The whole book is translated: the prefaces of Bernoulli and Klages (1925), the key to the plates, the Übersicht, the index (headwords translated, German order kept) and the plates. Google's OCR garbles the polytonic Greek, so the Greek was retyped from the scan.

Greek and Latin quotations are kept untranslated. Where the print has a non-word misprint in Greek or Latin, the standard text is given; where it has a real word or variant reading, the print is kept. The printer's signature numerals (every 16 pages) are omitted in the English. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
