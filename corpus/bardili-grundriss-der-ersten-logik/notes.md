## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Stuttgart 1800 print, Fraktur, colour camera scan from the Bavarian State Library (MDZ), 385 pages, no text layer; kept: title, dedication, preface and pp. 1–360 with the Nachtrag. Latin is kept with an [English] gloss, Greek as printed, quotations (Kant, Reinhold, Fichte…) are translated afresh with the standard Kant terms. The printer's catchword at the end of every 16-page gathering is normalised to the first English word of the next page. The German in this file is OCR of the scan, left uncorrected; where it and the scan differ, the scan in the PDF edition is the authority. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Editorial policies
**Languages.** Latin phrases and sentences are kept in the original with an [English] gloss in brackets; bare technical terms (such as *medius*, *prius*, *tacite*) are left unglossed, and on the display formulas of pp. 58–62 the glosses are omitted for reasons of space. Greek is kept as printed. Quotations from Kant, Reinhold, Fichte and others are translated afresh, using the standard English Kant terms, and French is glossed in brackets.

**Terminology.** Bardili's coinages and puns are rendered by fixed equivalents. *Daseyn* is "existence" and *Dortseyn* is "being-there" (the pair "Daseyn, Dortseyn" is "being-here, being-there"), and *Seyn* is "being". *Hineinbildung* is "imagining-into". *Mannigfaltigkeit* is "multiplicity". *Materiatur* is "materiature", parallel to *Sinnenwerk* "sense-work" and *Vernunftwerk* "work of reason". *Monstriren* and *demonstriren* are "monstrate" and "demonstrate", so that the pun survives. *Wechsel* is "alternation", *Wandel* "change" and *Veränderung* "alteration"; *vertilgen* is "eradicate" and *zernichten* "annihilate". *Gewahrnehmung* is "apprehension", and *Nebeneinander* and *Nacheinander* are "beside-one-another" and "after-one-another". *Wesen* is "essence" (in "essence of essences") but "being" for the organic being. The hyphenated *Ur-sache* is rendered plain "cause" (or "cause (*Ur-sache*)" where the pun matters), and the other coinages follow the same method: "Heerberge zum Ich" is the inn "At the I", "Weltseelenschild" "world-soul sign", "Pacisccenten" "paciscents", "Vernünfteley" "ratiocination" (distinct from "sophistry"), and "Welteinbildung" "world-imagination". Reinhold's and Kiesewetter's names are modernised in the English (Nikolai becomes Nicolai, Mendelsohn Mendelssohn, Epikur Epicurus); Jakob is kept as "Jakob".

**Emphasis.** Letter-spaced words in the Fraktur text (Sperrdruck) are rendered as emphasis; whole paragraphs spaced throughout (for example §. 12 and §. 14) are emphasised in full, and Antiqua words in Latin are italicised. Because ordinary justification also spreads letters in this print, borderline words were judged on the images, and a few short spans may differ from the print.

**Typography of the print.** The minus sign is printed variously as a hyphen, an en dash or an em dash. All are normalised to a true minus, "B − B" and "−B", except at the places noted below. Printer's signatures (A, B, … Z, and "2" for the second leaf, in Fraktur) are kept at the foot of the pages where they were read. The catchword printed at the foot of the last page of each 16-page gathering is normalised to the first English word of the following page, which may differ from the German word division. Pencil marks and a library stamp on the scans were ignored.

**Page breaks.** A hyphenated word or a clause that runs across a page break is finished on the page where it begins, so the English page boundary can differ by a word or two from the German. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
