## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Aesthetica, vol. I (1750) and vol. II (1758), Internet Archive scans of the Bavarian State Library copy. Vol. I: printed p = scan − 12 (the print's own folios are misnumbered on pp. 43–66; the markers follow the scan order). Vol. II: printed p = scan + 392 (the last page is misfolioed 623 for 624). The print skips § 594 (593 → 595); this is the print, not a loss. The 1750/1758 orthography is kept (v/u, i/j), the long s printed s, misprints are marked [sic: …]; the apostrophus dates (cIↃIↃCCLVIII) are written MDCCLVIII. Three suspected misprints were confirmed on the scan: Oeteam / Aeetam, Inu. / Iuu., and Sect. XXXIII / XXXXIII.

Terms: sensitivus = sensate, venustas = charm, nitor = brilliance, fucus = cosmetic, horridus = rough, tenue = slender, tumor = tumidity, sententia (gnomic) = maxim, studium veritatis = pursuit of truth, fabulae implicitae = complex fables, cognitio media = middle cognition; βάθος is left as [bathos]. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
