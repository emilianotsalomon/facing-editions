## General remark — Editorial policies
Printed page = scan page minus one; the blank versos of pp. 18 and 120 are omitted. Spaced type (*Sperrdruck*) is rendered in italics, and the italicised span is the one actually spaced in the print: where only an ordinal is spaced ("das *zweite* Hauptstück") only the ordinal is italicised, and where a word is plain in the print (for example "Lehrbuche" and "obersten") no emphasis is given even if a quotation or parallel passage has it. Spacing inside Latin or other roman-type quotations is mentioned in the gloss, since italics cannot be nested there. Quotation marks, the quoted section references ("§. 55.") and the author's punctuation are kept as printed; the sheet lines and star signatures (for example "Beurtheilende Uebersicht üb. Bolzanos Wissenschaftslehre ꝛc.", given as "… Theory of Science, etc.") are reproduced, and catchwords are not.

Terms follow the Rusnock–George renderings of Bolzano's *Wissenschaftslehre* (*Erkenntnislehre* = Theory of Knowledge, and so on) and these decisions: *Hauptstück* = "chapter"; *Haupttheil* = "main part"; *Abtheilung* = "division"; *Abschnitt* = "section"; *Lehrbegriff* = "doctrinal system"; *sittliche Zuträglichkeit* = "moral utility" (plain *Nutzen* = "use"); *Tugendmittel* = "means of virtue"; *Heiligungsmittel* = "means of sanctification"; *Anwartschaft* = "prospect"; *Asketik* = "ascetics"; *Moral* = "morals"; *Gebrechlichkeitssünde*, *läßliche Sünde*, *Todsünde* = "sin of frailty", "venial sin", "mortal sin"; *Verdienst* = "merit"; *Schuld* = "guilt"; *Schuldigkeit* = "obligation"; *Gewissensrath* = "counselor in matters of conscience"; *Beseligung* = "bestowal of blessedness"; *Weltgericht* = "Last Judgment"; *Selbstbeherrschung* = "self-control". "Vf." in the print is the author, and "B." is kept as "B." Titles of Bolzano's own works are translated (for example *Ansichten eines freisinnigen Theologen über Kirche und Staat*, Sulzbach 1834, as *Views of a Liberal-Minded Theologian on Church and State*), but the title of a journal is left in German. Greek lettering and Greek words are kept as printed. Anglo-American spelling is used. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
