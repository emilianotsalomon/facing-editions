## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Cesena 1780, HathiTrust/Google PDF of the Complutense University copy (335 pages). The folded map (scan page 43) is replaced by the full sheet from the Wellcome Library copy; the genealogy leaf (scan page 325) also comes from the Wellcome copy, with the English tree set by hand; pages 302–309 of the scan are misbound and were put in order. The marginal § notes and "Lib. I." marginalia of the print are kept in the margin. Cullen's 1787 English was used as a reference for terms and sense, never copied; where Clavigero sets a clause in italics, Robertson's English is kept. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
