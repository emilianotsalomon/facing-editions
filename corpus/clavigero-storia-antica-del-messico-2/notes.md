## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Books VI–VII, Cesena 1780, HathiTrust/Google PDF of the Complutense copy (323 pages). Fifteen leaves come from the Wellcome Library copy: the twelve folded plates and three other leaves. The Wellcome plates stand in a different order from those of the Complutense copy and were matched by looking at them. The Complutense scan shows the leaves of printed pp. 150 and 218 twice, half covered by a plate flap. A hyphenated catchword syllable ("se-") is repeated whole on the next page, and the word belongs to the next page. Cullen's 1787 English was a reference only. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
