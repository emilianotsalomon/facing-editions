## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Books VIII–X (the conquest), Cesena 1780, HathiTrust/Google PDF of the Complutense copy (277 pages). The folded map was scanned blank side up, and the Wellcome copy has none: it is omitted. The portrait plate and printed p. 9 (hidden under the plate flap in the Complutense scan) come from the Wellcome copy. The volume ends with the genealogies of Moctezuma and Cortés, the index of Tomo III, the general alphabetical index of the whole history (Italian order kept, headwords translated, references as printed) and the Errata with the printer's notice. The foot-line title varies in the print ("Storia del Messico" / "Storia Antica del Messico") and is followed as printed. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
