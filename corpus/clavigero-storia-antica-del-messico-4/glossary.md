# Glossary and policies — Clavigero, *Storia antica del Messico*, tomo 4 → *Ancient History of Mexico*

Decisions recorded during production; partial. The policies below were common to all four volumes.

- **Reference translation**: Cullen's English of 1787 was used for terms and as a check on the sense, never copied.
- **Quotations**: footnote quotations are translated into English; where Clavigero sets a clause in italics, Robertson's English is kept.
- **Names**: Italianised Spanish given names are given in their Spanish forms; the Italian *Mori* of Africans is rendered "Blacks".
- **Capitals**: period capitals of common nouns are lowercased.
- **Catchwords**: a hyphenated catchword syllable ("se-") is repeated whole on the next page; the word belongs to the next page.
- **Indexes**: Italian alphabetical order kept, headwords translated, references as printed.
- **Foot-line title**: follows the print page by page ("Storia del Messico" / "Storia Antica del Messico").
