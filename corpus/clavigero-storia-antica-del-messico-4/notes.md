## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Dissertations, Cesena 1781, HathiTrust/Google PDF of the Complutense copy (345 pages; the text begins at scan page 8, with an offset of +7). Pages without text were dropped. Footnote quotations are translated into English; Cullen's 1787 English was a reference for terms and sense, never copied. The printer mixes stray italic letters, which are ignored in the English. The Italian *Mori* of Africans is rendered "Blacks", and Italianised Spanish given names are given in their Spanish forms. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
