# Dilthey, *Die Jugendgeschichte Hegels* (Berlin 1905) — binding glossary & policies

## Policies (the user chose these)
- **Quotations from Hegel's manuscripts, letters (Hölderlin, Schelling, Hegel), Kant, Schiller, Fichte, Schelling,
  Schleiermacher, the Bible, etc.**: translate FRESH from the German as Dilthey prints it, with the standard
  Hegel/Kant terms below. Do NOT copy published English translations (Knox, *Early Theological Writings*, etc.).
  Keep Dilthey's cuts (… or —) exactly.
- **Greek / Latin / French** words or phrases: keep as printed (Latin/French italic only if printed italic) and add an
  English gloss in [square brackets] only when a reader without the language would lose the sense.
- **Book titles**: the print sets them in roman, without italics — keep them roman. Well-known works under their usual
  English title (Phenomenology, Critique of Pure Reason, Spinoza's Ethics, Lessing's Nathan, the Antigone,
  Schleiermacher's Speeches); scholarly literature cited by author+title (Rosenkranz, Hegels Leben; Haym, Hegel und
  seine Zeit; Mollat edition) keeps the GERMAN title.
- Names as printed (already modern). Aesculapius, Socrates, Plato, Sophocles in English form.
- **American spelling** (honor, labor, center, -ize). »…« → “…”, inner ›…‹ or ‚…‘ → ‘…’. Em dash — without spaces.
- Dates: “27. August 1770” → 27 August 1770; “18. Jahrhundert” → eighteenth century; “im Herbst 1796” → in the autumn of 1796.
- Divine pronouns: lower case (he, his), as the print has them lower case.
- **Emphasis**: the print's only emphasis is Sperrdruck (letter-spaced type) → `<span class="sp">…</span>` around the
  English equivalent. Italic in the print → `<em>`. NOTE: many lines look patchily BOLD — that is uneven inking in
  the scan, NOT bold type: ignore it.
- Abbreviations: S. = p. / pp.; Z. = l. (line); v. o. = from top; v. u. = from bot.; Bl. = leaf / leaves; Bd. = vol.;
  Ms./Mss. kept; vgl. = cf.; ff. kept; u. s. w. = etc.; s. = see. Manuscript sigla (VII 127b, XI 39a, XIII 11–51) as printed.
