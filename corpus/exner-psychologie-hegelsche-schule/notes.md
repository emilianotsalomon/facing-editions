## General remark — Editorial policies
The errata printed at the end of each Heft (*Verbesserungen*) have been applied in both columns, German and English, except where noted. Their line references are sometimes ambiguous ("l. 12" may count from the top or, as often here, "von unten" from the bottom); where the line counted from the top contained nothing to correct, the reading from the bottom was adopted, and the note on the page records this. Misprints that are not in the errata are corrected silently only when they are evident non-words; real words and odd grammatical forms are kept as printed, and the English renders their sense. Spellings of the period (*lößt*, *Schluße*, *Pflegmatiker*, *mehre*, *deßhalb*, *unwillkührlich*, *Dieß*) are kept. Emphasis follows the *Sperrdruck* of the print, checked against the page images; words set in Antiqua (Latin and French words, e.g. *terminus medius*, *Corpus delicti*, *bona fide*) are plain in the German and italic in the English. Latin, Greek and Italian phrases are kept and glossed in square brackets on first occurrence. Quotations: Exner often paraphrases Michelet, Erdmann and Rosenkranz without quotation marks, and none are added. A quotation within a quotation is printed with doubled marks („„…““) in the German and set as ‘…’ within “…” in the English; in a multi-paragraph quotation the English opens each paragraph with “ and closes only at the end. Names are modernised in the English (Leibniz, Wolff, Camões, Herder, Chalybäus), while the German keeps the printed forms. Fixed renderings beyond the glossary: *Darstellung* "exposition" (to keep "presentation" for *Vorstellung*); *verständig* "of the understanding" and *Verständigkeit* "understanding-mindedness"; *sittlich* "moral"; *Ineinander* "within-one-another" and *Außereinander* "asunderness"; *Insichsein* "being-within-itself"; *Erschleichung* "surreptitious substitution", "inference" or "move", according to context; *Herr / Knecht* "lord / bondsman" (Hegel) but "master" where Exner speaks empirically; *Herrschaft und Knechtschaft* "lordship and bondage"; *Hellsehen* "clairvoyance"; *Ahnung* "presentiment"; *Seelenkrankheiten* "diseases of the soul"; *Faselei* "drivel"; *Anstoßen* "push"; *Auflösung* "dissolution" (of a concept into another) but "resolution" of differences; *Empirie* "empirical knowledge". The opening pages of Heft I (p. 1) and Heft II (p. 1) carry no folio, and the Errata pages of each Heft are unnumbered; the Errata of Heft II is reproduced with the print's ditto dashes expanded editorially ("S.", "Z.", "lies:").
