# GLOSSARY and policies — Boutroux, *La Psychologie du mysticisme* (1902)
Binding for translators and reviewers.

## Title
*La Psychologie du mysticisme* → *The Psychology of Mysticism*. Sections I, II, III stay bare Roman numerals.

## Policies
- **Quotations.** English authors in the original English as printed (Longfellow's couplet stays exactly as printed in BOTH columns). Others (Pascal, Hugo, Goethe's prose paraphrase, mystics) translated fresh from Boutroux's French. English quotation marks “…” in the English; « … » as printed in the French, opening and closing once, not at every line.
- **Verse** (indented couplets): one block, class `v`, the whole couplet in `<em>` as printed, lines separated by ` / ` (space slash space). German or other non-English verse: in the English column keep the original lines, then ` / ` and an English rendering in square brackets, roman: `<em>Das Ewig-Weibliche / Zieht uns hinan.</em> / [The Eternal-Feminine / Draws us upward.]`
- **Greek / Latin**: kept as printed + [English gloss] in square brackets in the English.
- **Names** modernised in the English only: Gœthe → Goethe, Plotin → Plotinus, Socrate → Socrates, François d'Assise → Francis of Assisi, saint Bernard → Saint Bernard, sainte Thérèse → Saint Teresa, saint Jean de la Croix → Saint John of the Cross, Jacob Bœhme → Jacob Boehme, Maître Eckhart → Meister Eckhart, Ruysbroeck → Ruusbroec, Mme Guyon → Madame Guyon. French column: exactly as printed.
- **Divine pronouns**: lower case (he, him), as the French.
- **Spelling**: American. Dates: "November 23, 1654".
- **Misprints**: French column as printed (incl. "raye", "par dessus"), EXCEPT words the printer ran together or split (`dessensations` → `des sensations`, `cerveau.Mais` → `cerveau. Mais`) and evident non-words (`dn` → `du`). A wrong fact (p. 6 "1664" for Pascal's 1654) is kept in the French and set in the English as `1664 [recte 1654]`. List every such case in your notes file.
- **French typography** in the French column: the print's space before ; : ? ! and inside « » is kept as a no-break space (U+00A0). Apostrophe ’ in the English, ' as printed in the French.
- Running heads (folios) are written as printed; the retypeset edition drops them.
