# GLOSSARY and policies — Exner, *Die Psychologie der Hegelschen Schule* (1842/1844)
Binding for translators and reviewers. Franz Exner (1802–1853, Prague) is a Herbartian; he attacks the Hegelian
psychologies of Karl Rosenkranz, Karl Ludwig Michelet and Johann Eduard Erdmann (Heft I, 1842), then answers the
replies of Rosenkranz and Erdmann (Heft II, 1844). Tone: sharp, ironic, polemical — keep the irony and the edge.

## Title and parts
- *Die Psychologie der Hegelschen Schule* → *The Psychology of the Hegelian School*; „beurtheilt von" → "examined by".
- Heft I (1842) = "Part I"; „Zweites Heft: Die Erwiderungen der Herren K. Rosenkranz und J. E. Erdmann" →
  "Part Two: The Replies of Messrs. K. Rosenkranz and J. E. Erdmann".
- Vorbemerkung → Preliminary Remark; Verbesserungen → Errata; Persönliches → Personal Matters;
  Weitere Charakterisirung der Widerlegung → Further Characterization of the Refutation;
  Die Haupteinwendungen gegen die Hegelsche Psychologie → The Principal Objections to the Hegelian Psychology.
  Other headings: translate plainly, same style.

## Policies
- Quotations: Exner quotes Rosenkranz, Michelet, Erdmann, Hegel, Herbart — all German: translate fresh in standard
  Hegel terminology (above). Keep every quotation complete, inside “…”. Nested quotations ‘…’.
- Book titles quoted in the text: translate into English inside “…” (the German column has the original).
  Journals: *Jahrbücher für wissenschaftliche Kritik*, *Allgemeine Zeitung*, Cotta's *Deutsche Vierteljahrsschrift* —
  keep German name, italic.
- Latin / French / Greek in the text (a priori, prius, principium exclusi tertii, Grimassiers): kept as printed,
  italic in the English; add [English gloss] in brackets only on the first occurrence of a non-obvious phrase.
- Names: modernised in the English only — Platon → Plato, Musmann → Mussmann, Weiße → Weisse, Göschel, Daub,
  Herbart, Drobisch, Michelet, Erdmann as usual. „H. Pr. R." / „Hr. Prof. R." → "Prof. R."; „des Hn. Prs. R." →
  "Prof. R.'s" / "of Prof. R."; „H. Pr. E." → "Prof. E."; „Herr Exner", „Herr Dr. Weiße" → "Herr Exner",
  "Dr. Weisse".
- References: „S. 370" → "p. 370"; „S. 411 u. w." → "pp. 411 ff."; „2. Ausg." → "2nd ed."; „vgl." → "cf.";
  „a. a. O." → "loc. cit."; „§ 54" stays; „Anm." → "Remark" (Hegel's Anmerkung) or "note".
- Capitalisation: English norms. Spelling: American. Quoted „Sie"/„Ihr" etc.: ordinary English "you".
- German column (`orig/`): orthography exactly as printed (Dieß, Willkühr, Consequenzen, Ueberzeugung, Hn. Prs.,
  seyn/sein as printed); long ſ → s; Fraktur `‚‚`/`,,` → „ and closing “ ; apostrophes ’.
- Misprints: an evident non-word → corrected silently in both columns; the ERRATA below are applied silently in
  both columns (that is the reader's text); anything else doubtful → kept, noted in your notes file.
- Signatures (1*, 3 …), catchwords, folios: not reproduced (the retypeset edition marks pages in the margin).
