# Glossary and policies — Rosenkranz, *G. W. F. Hegel's Leben* (Berlin 1844) → *The Life of G. W. F. Hegel*

BINDING for translators and reviewers. Where this file is silent, choose the plain scholarly English a careful
translator of Hegel (Miller, Pinkard, Knox, Harris, Cambridge Hegel Translations) would use, and note the decision.

## General policies
- **Register**: Rosenkranz writes a learned, warm, often rhetorical 1840s biography. Keep his long periods where
  English bears them, his antitheses, dashes and exclamations. No modernising paraphrase, no condensing.
- **Sperrdruck** (letter-spaced words) → `<em>`, exactly where printed (names included: the print spaces many names).
  Roman-type (Antiqua) foreign words in a German sentence: Latin/French/Italian words → `<em>`.
- **Latin**: short phrases and titles kept in Latin (`<em>`), followed by `[English]` the first time on a page when
  the sense is not obvious (*capita repraesentativa* [representative heads]). **Whole Latin sentences or entries**
  (Hegel's Latin diary, Latin theses, Latin letters): translate into English and put `<span class="lat">[Latin]</span>`
  at the start of each Latin stretch (see model s483). Same for long **French** passages: `<span class="lat">[French]</span>`.
  Greek: always kept exactly as printed (polytonic), with `[English]` gloss only if Rosenkranz does not explain it.
- **Quotations** (Hegel's own texts — diaries, fragments, letters, the *Phenomenology*, aphorisms — and Schelling,
  Hölderlin, Goethe, Schiller, Kant, Fichte, Jacobi…): translate FRESH from the German as Rosenkranz prints it, with
  the Hegel terms below. Do not paste published translations. Bible: fresh from the German wording printed.
  Quotation marks „…“ → “…”, inner ‚…‘ → ‘…’.
- **Verse** (Hegel's *Eleusis*, Förster's poem, Schiller, etc.): line-for-line, unrhymed, same number of lines,
  same indentation; see model s123.
- **Divine pronouns**: follow the print (Er → He, er → he).
- **Names**: modernised in the English (Würtemberg → Württemberg, Kannstadt → Cannstatt, Speier → Speyer,
  Göthe → Goethe, Strauß → Strauss, Voß → Voss, Liefland → Livonia, Nürnberg → Nuremberg, Groß-Bottwar → Großbottwar);
  Latinised personal names in Latin passages → vernacular (Clessius → Cless). Otherwise keep the printed spelling
  (Förster, Göschel, Marheineke, Niethammer, Creuzer, Daub, Gans, Hotho, Henning, Michelet, Hinrichs, Schulze,
  Altenstein, Steiger, Gogel, Sinclair, Zwilling, Paulus, Seebeck, Knebel, Frommann, Schwab, Varnhagen von Ense).
  Doubtful forms (e.g. "Regier" s476): keep as printed, note it.
- **"Herr"** before names is kept (Herr Schwab); "Herr Professor X" → Professor X; "Frau" kept.
- **Spelling**: American (honor, color, favor, defense, center, -ize), but keep "Hegel's Wastebook" (English in the print).
- **Dates**: "am 27. August 1770" → "on 27 August 1770"; diary headings "Sonntags den 26. Juni" →
  "Sunday, the 26th of June."; "Michaelis" → Michaelmas; "Ostern" → Easter.
- **References**: "S. 5" → "p. 5"; "Bd." → vol.; "a. a. O." → loc. cit.; "vgl." → cf.; "u. s. w." → etc.;
  "d. h." → i.e.; "z. B." → e.g.; "Hegel's Werke Bd. XVII" → "Hegel's *Works*, vol. XVII". Book titles in italics.
- **Money**: Gulden → florins (fl.), Thaler → thalers, Kreuzer → kreuzer, Louisd'or → louis d'or.
- **Errata** (printed list, s041): apply SILENTLY in the English on these pages (scan numbers):
  s085 Neislingen (not Meislingen) and Maulbronn (not Maulbram); s133 comma belongs after "steht", not after
  "Staates"; s193 "nun" (not "nur"); s256 Absoluten; s271 Richerand's; s346 "oder" (not "der");
  s365 insert "als ein" between "Alles" and "durch"; s378 comma after "Rechts"; s381 add "gegen ihn" after
  "Antipathie", and "verlangte" (not "verlangt"); s384 "Absoluten" capitalised; s396 Maseteren; s509 "des
  Richterthrones" (not "das Richterthronen").
