## Names and works
- Jacobi — Friedrich Heinrich Jacobi. *Von den göttlichen Dingen und ihrer Offenbarung* (1811) = *On Divine Things and Their Revelation*; *Jacobi an Fichte* (1799) = *Jacobi to Fichte*; *Briefwechsel* = *Correspondence*. In footnotes cite as printed but translated: "V. d. g. D. S. 152" → "*On Divine Things*, p. 152".
- Jacobi's "die Auswendigen" (those who are "outside", know only externally) → "the Outward Ones" (first occurrence: "the Outward Ones [die Auswendigen]").
- Hegel's *Encyclopädie* = *Encyclopaedia*; *Phänomenologie* = *Phenomenology*; *Wissenschaft der Logik* = *Science of Logic*.
- Hinrichs, *Die Religion im innern Verhältnisse zur Wissenschaft* (1822, foreword by Hegel) = *Religion in Its Inner Relation to Science*.
- Kant, *Muthmaßlicher Anfang der Menschengeschichte* (1786) = *Conjectural Beginning of Human History*.
- Biblical names in English forms: Paulus → Paul, Apollo → Apollos, Kephas → Cephas, Elim, Raphidim → Rephidim.
- Modernise names in the English only (the scan stays as printed).
