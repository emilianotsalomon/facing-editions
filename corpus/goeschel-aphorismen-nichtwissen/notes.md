## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Berlin 1829 print, Google Books Fraktur scan, 214 pages; publisher advertisements, the Google notice and blank pages are left out. The German in this file is OCR of the scan, left uncorrected; where it and the scan differ, the scan in the PDF edition is the authority. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Editorial policies
Divine pronouns follow the print: lower case where the print has *er*, *ihm*, *sein*, *sich*, *selbst* for God, Christ or the Spirit, and capitals where it has *Er*, *Ihm*, *Ihn*, *Seiner*, *Sich Selbst*. Latin is kept as printed (in italics) with the English in brackets; Greek is kept, with accents supplied where the print has none. Words the print letter-spaces (Sperrdruck) are italicised, and only the words actually spaced. Titles in source references are translated ("S." becomes "p."), and source lines after quotations which have no footnote mark or rule in the print are set as plain lines, not as footnotes. Names are modernised in a few places (Zacharias = Zechariah; the print's "Heinrich Friedrich Jacobi" = Friedrich Heinrich Jacobi). Printed catchwords at page feet are not reproduced, except where they contain a misprint. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Terms
Fixed renderings include *Entäußerung* = externalization (*Selbstentäußerung* = self-emptying), *Urbild / Abbild* = archetype / copy (*Bild / Ebenbild* = image / likeness), *Gemeine* = community, *Gemeinschaft* = communion, *Knecht* = bondsman, *Nichtige* = the null (nugatory), *Unwissenheit* = ignorance, *Willkühr* = arbitrariness, *Bestimmung* of man = vocation, *Schwärmer* = enthusiast, *Fürsichseyn / Ansichseyn / Anundfürsichseyn* = being-for-itself / in-itself / in-and-for-itself, *Verstand* = the understanding (*Verständniß* = comprehension), *Dreyeinigkeit* = Triunity (*Dreyfaltigkeit* = threefoldness, p. 153), *Unerkenntlichkeit* = uncognizability. The Hegelian *Dieser* is "This" (also "sublated This", pp. 59, 61, 72; "Thises", p. 93); "this one" would be an alternative. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Title page and epigraphs
A handwritten line on the title page gives the author's office as "Oberlandgerichtsrath in Naumburg" ("Higher Regional Court Councillor in Naumburg"). On the unnumbered epigraph page the print reads "leere, lose Abkehr" in Colossians 2:8, not Luther's "lose Verführung"; this was checked at high magnification and is rendered "empty, vain turning-away" (the same words recur at p. 179). — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## [p. 9] The Outward, Inner and Immanent Ones
