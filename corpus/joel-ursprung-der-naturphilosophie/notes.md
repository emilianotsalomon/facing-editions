## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: the Google scan of the University of California copy (Antiqua, 191 pages); printed p = scan − 19, prefaces i–viii. Scan pages 18–19 are a bad duplicate of 20–21 and were skipped. Page 28 is cropped in the scan and was completed from the 1906 edition (Internet Archive, derursprungderna00jo). The notes are endnotes 1–20 (pp. 151–158), whose superscript cues the OCR lost; they were restored. The German was built by script from the text layer and corrected against the images (the "correction method"). — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
