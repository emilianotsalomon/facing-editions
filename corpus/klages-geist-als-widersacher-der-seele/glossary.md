# GLOSSARY — Klages, *Der Geist als Widersacher der Seele* (Leipzig 1929–1932)
The renderings the translation uses, recorded after the fact. The translation was made in eight batches (Sept 2026) and is **unrevised**, so drift remains in places (see the end).

## Title and parts
*Der Geist als Widersacher der Seele* → *Spirit as Adversary of the Soul*. Band → Volume; Buch → Book; Kapitel → Chapter ("1. Kapitel — …" → "Chapter 1 — …"); Rückblick → Retrospect; Anmerkungen zum I. Band → Notes to Volume I.
Abschnitt → **Part** in Books I–IV, but **Section** in Book V, because Book V has its own Teile (Erster Teil: Die Lehre → Part One: The Doctrine; Zweiter Teil: Das Weltbild des Pelasgertums → Part Two: The World-Picture of Pelasgianism).

## Known drift (unrevised)
- **Wirklichkeit → "reality"** survives in about 60 places, mostly Book II and the last batch of Book V (the last batch was normalised to "actuality" once, 131 places). Read "reality" as "actuality" unless the German has Realität.
- Abschnitt is "Part" in Books I–IV and "Section" in Book V (deliberate, see above).
- Erlebnis and Erfahrung both come out as "experience" in places; check the German.
