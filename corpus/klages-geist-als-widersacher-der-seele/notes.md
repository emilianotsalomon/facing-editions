## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: scanned in parts (a 1960-page parallel PDF). In the scan of the second part, p. 70 (the opening of chapter 9, "Die Gegensätzlichkeit von Seele und Geist") is missing and a duplicate of p. 66 stands in its place; pp. 71–76 were supplied separately. The scan of Band III has swapped page pairs (pp. 1267/1268 and neighbours), which were put in order by the printed folios. Spaced type (Sperrsatz) is rendered in italics. The English of *Wirklichkeit* is "actuality" throughout. The endnotes are set under their cue paragraphs. The original prints "Anmerkung 4ü" (kept). — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
