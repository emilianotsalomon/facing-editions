## General remark — Editorial policies
**Typography.** Rosenkranz's Fraktur text spaces its emphasised words letter by letter (Sperrdruck); the English renders these as emphasis. Because justification in Fraktur also spreads letters, only word- or phrase-level spacing that stood out against normally set lines was marked, so a few faint or borderline cases (for example pp. 89–90 and 95–98) may be under- or over-marked. Foreign words and names set in Antiqua are italicised, and book titles are italicised.

**Foreign languages.** Latin, French and Greek are handled as follows. Short phrases stay in the original with an [English] gloss. Longer stretches (Hegel's Latin diary and Latin essays from p. 431 on, the Latin theses, Cousin's and Willm's French, Pfaff's Latin salutations) are translated and tagged [Latin] or [French]. Greek is kept as printed. Passages quoted by Rosenkranz from Hegel, Schelling and others are translated afresh from the German, without recourse to earlier English versions.

**Names and terms.** Archaic spellings are modernised: Würtemberg, Göthe, Baiern, Macchiavell, Cartesius, Leibnitz and Wolf become Württemberg, Goethe, Bavaria, Machiavelli, Descartes, Leibniz and Wolff; Töplitz becomes Teplitz and Heß becomes Hess. Names the print gives in an odd form are kept as printed where no correction could be shown (for example Cloß, Sietze, Xeller, Rösel, Leypold, Duttenhofer, Süßkind, Cöln). "Herr" is dropped before titles. Fichte's *Wissenschaftslehre* is rendered *Science of Knowledge* throughout. Hegel's *Begriff* is "Concept" in its technical sense and "concept" in ordinary use. *Aufhebung* is "sublation", *Anschauung* "intuition", *Sinnlichkeit* "sensibility". References to "S. W." are given as *Works*, vol. N. Dates follow the pattern "6 July 1785", and spelling is American (skepticism).

**Fraktur x and r.** In Fraktur the letters x and r are easily confused, and several readings depend on this. Troxler (p. 322), Alexanderplatz (p. 325), Quenstedt (p. 344), Sextus Pompeius (p. 452), "Excipiren" (pp. 448–451), Xeller (p. 362) and d'Yxkull (pp. 301–304, 408) are read as x where the printed letter looks like r.

**Headings.** Chapter titles and running heads are translated as printed. Where the printed head and the Contents differ slightly, the Contents wording is used in many places (for example "Move to Prussia", "Hegel's Contributions to the Berlin Yearbooks"). The comma in heads such as "Hegel's Wastebook, 1803–1806" and "Critical Journal of Philosophy, 1802–1803" is not printed consistently and has not been fully harmonised.

**Page breaks.** A sentence that runs across a page break is completed on the English page where the German word begins, and hyphenated words are finished on the page where they start, so the English page boundary can differ by a word or two from the German. Printers' signatures (gathering marks such as "2" and "2*") are kept at the foot of the pages where they were read. The errata printed on p. XXXII were applied silently.

**Missing material.** The frontispiece portrait promised on the title page is missing from the scan (the scan reads "PAGE NOT AVAILABLE" at that point), so it is not reproduced. Blank leaves and the versos of the book dividers are omitted. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
