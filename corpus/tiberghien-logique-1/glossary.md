# Glossary and policies — Tiberghien, *Logique*, tome I (1865) → *Logic*

Decisions recorded during production; partial.

- **Terminology source**: the glossary was built from period English translations (Henry/Wight's Cousin, Hamilton, Meiklejohn).
- **Quotations**: Reid, Hamilton and Locke are restored in their original English.
- **Terms**: *esprit* in the Krause triad = "spirit"; *conscience* in the moral sense = "conscience" (not "consciousness").
- **God pronouns**: lower case mid-sentence.
- **Catchwords/signature lines**: signature lines keep their numeral ("LA LOGIQUE I 31").
