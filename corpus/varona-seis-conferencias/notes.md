## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Barcelona [1887], HathiTrust copy of the Google scan (University of Texas, Austin copy), 205 pages; blank pages are left out. English-language authors quoted by Varona (Emerson and others) are given in their original English where it could be found, otherwise translated afresh from Varona's Spanish; Latin is kept with a gloss; names are modernised in the English only. The Spanish text of the page is the scan. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Editorial policies
Quotations from Emerson and other English-language authors are restored in their original English (checked verbatim against the Project Gutenberg texts); quotations from other languages, and English passages whose source could not be found, are translated afresh from Varona's Spanish. Latin phrases are kept and glossed in square brackets; names are modernised (Marino, Brantôme, Tommasina Spinola, Giuliano de' Medici, Sainte-Beuve, Jean Paul Richter, Harriet Martineau, Nilsson, Nicosthenes, Euphronios, Cnidus, Scaean gates, Ilium); American spelling is used. Titles of works are given in their usual original or English form (*Les Contemplations*, *Les Châtiments*, *Notre-Dame de Paris*, *Les Misérables*, *La Légende des siècles*, *Tristia*, *Exemplary Novels*, *Journey to Parnassus*, *Monk in Prayer*, *Night Watch*), and italics follow the print. Where a Spanish word is hyphenated across a page break, it is completed on the page where it begins, so that English sentences may break at a different point from the Spanish. Printer's signature numerals, library numbers and stray marks are ignored. Terms: "pontones" "hulks", "sambenito" "sanbenito" (not italic in the print), "consejero general" "councilor-general", "clarines" "clarions", Louis Napoleon Bonaparte (print "Luis Napoleón"); *espíritu* is "mind" for intellectual matters ("world of the mind", "solvent of the mind") and "spirit" for moral or collective ones ("human spirit", "communion of spirits"), which a reader may wish to harmonise; *señores* alone is "gentlemen"; *preciosa* / *preciosismo* are *précieuse* / *préciosité*; *galantería* is "gallantry", *cortesía* "courtesy"; *la moral* is "morality". The printed folios of the openings of the six lectures (pp. 5, 29, 55, 97, 127, 149) were checked.
