## General remark — Provenance of the edition
Source: Halle 1720, Bavarian State Library colour scan (657 pages); two rescanned duplicate pages were dropped. The printer's folios are erratic; the markers follow the folios as printed. Index entries carry no bracketed German. The German in this file is OCR of the scan, left uncorrected; where it and the scan differ, the scan in the PDF edition is the authority. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — Editorial policies
The English follows the German page by page; the printed folio, section number (§.), running head and printer's signature are given as printed, including misprints, which are noted below at the place where they occur. The folios of this copy are genuinely erratic (numbers repeat, jump, or restart), so the page headings in these notes are the numbers as printed and, where one number occurs twice, say which occurrence is meant. Two pages that the scan contains twice (rescans of the same printed pages) are given only once. Words divided between two pages stand on the page where they begin. Marginal notes (Wolff's glosses) are translated and, where they run on across a page break, are split as in the print. Handwritten Latin notes, underlinings and library marks in the scanned copy, and show-through from the verso, are not part of the printed text and are ignored.

Words that Wolff sets in bold type as defined terms are marked in italics; titles of his own works are italicised (*Thoughts on the Powers of the Understanding*, i.e. the German *Logic*; *Reasonable Thoughts on Nature*), and Latin or French words and titles set in Antiqua remain in Latin or French. The Preface is unpaginated, is dated Halle, 23 December 1719, and is followed by the Notice, paginated from 4. The names are modernised where the print has Latinised forms (Euclides is Euclid, Hugenius is Huygens, Titius, Caius, Sempronius).

Technical terms follow one glossary: *zureichender Grund* is "sufficient reason"; *Eigenschafft* "attribute"; *erfinden* "invent" and *Erfindungs-Kunst* "art of inventing"; *Einbildungs-Krafft* "imagination" (*Einbildung*, "image of the imagination"); *Erklärung* "definition"; *vollständig* "adequate"; *Vergnügen* "satisfaction" and *Lust* "pleasure"; *Gliedmassen* "organs"; *ausdehnende Krafft* "elastic force"; *Einheiten* "unities"; *figürliche Erkäntnis* "symbolic cognition"; *natürlicher Einfluß* "physical influx"; *Erkäntnis GOttes* "knowledge of God"; *freywillig* "voluntary" as against *frey* "free"; the marginal *Erinnerung* "Remark"; *Krafft* "force"; *Thun* "doing". Where a note below records a rendering, the decision is local to the passage. — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026

## General remark — The two Indexes
The Indexes carry no printed folios. The First Index gives Wolff's German terms with their Latin equivalents; the headwords follow the book's glossary ("Thing subsisting by itself" for *Vor sich bestehendes Ding*, "Organs of the senses" for *Gliedmaßen*). Entries that run on across a page, as in the print, are left running on. Some headwords were misread in the print and were checked against the text: the entry read "Fisch, 214" is *Falsch* ("False", the true and the false of §. 395); *Gefühl* is "Touch"; *Wiedrige Affecten* are "Disagreeable affects", the counterpart of "Angenehme Affecten"; *Schadenfroh* is "Malicious"; *Beyfallen* is "Occurring to one" (uncertain); *Ergründen* is "Fathoming"; *Besinnen* "recollection". Several page numbers are blurred or cut by the margin and were read as best possible (for instance 520/521 under *Allwissenheit*, "130" under *Aufmerksamkeit*, and 23 under the entry for composite things); an entry such as Matter "345. 336" is printed 336, not 346. The Index closes with "Ende des Registerg.", a misprint rendered "End of the Index." — prepared from the production records, Oct 2026
